Career Compass should feel like a conversation, not a survey.
Questions should be tied to the user's chosen path whenever possible.
Answers should reveal motivations without making the scoring obvious.
Each answer should map to one and only one user profile category.
The six core motivators are:
Income
Work_life_balance
Purpose
Job_security
Growth
Well-being
Users do not need to know the categories being measured.
Different paths can measure the same motivator from different perspectives.
Focus on discovering why the user wants something, not just what they want.
